Frequently Asked Questions

Real questions from international BYD dealers

Q: What is the minimum order for BYD export from China?
Minimum 4 units per 40ft container. Mixed models allowed. Single-unit orders not available.
Q: How much does it cost to ship a BYD from China to Australia?
Approximately RMB 5,000-8,000 (AUD 1,064-1,702) per unit via RoRo shipping. Transit time 14-18 days. Zero import duty under China-Australia FTA.
Q: Which BYD models are exclusive to China?
Qin Plus DM-i and Song Pro DM-i are NOT officially sold in Australia, Thailand, or Brazil. These are exclusive supply opportunities with no local competition.
Q: What documents are provided for export?
Commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin, export license, and vehicle conformity certificate.
Q: Can I mix different BYD models in one container?
Yes. A standard 40ft container can hold 4 vehicles. You can mix Atto 2, Atto 3, Seal 6, and other models in a single shipment.
Q: What is the BYD Shark 6 price for export?
Shark 6 pricing is negotiated separately due to high demand. The Australia retail price is AUD $57,900-$71,990. Contact us for wholesale export pricing.
